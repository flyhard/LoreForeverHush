# Changelog

## 1.1.0

First public release.

- Lore Forever pauses while Spoken reads a line, and continues about 1.5 seconds after Spoken goes quiet.
- A Lore Forever story that starts by itself while Spoken is talking is paused too.
- Stories recorded in pieces continue from the piece they stopped in. A story recorded as one file starts over, because WoW cannot start a sound partway through.
- `/lfhush` turns it on or off (`/lfhush on`, `/lfhush off`).
- Loads only when both Spoken and Lore Forever are installed and enabled.
