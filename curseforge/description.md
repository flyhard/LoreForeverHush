**Lore Forever and Spoken, without talking over each other.**

If you run both [Lore Forever](https://www.curseforge.com/wow/addons/lore-forever) and [Spoken](https://www.curseforge.com/wow/addons/spoken), a zone story can keep playing while a quest giver speaks. Lore Forever Hush pauses Lore Forever while Spoken reads a line, and lets it continue once Spoken is done.

## What it does

- When Spoken starts a line, Lore Forever's narration pauses.
- About 1.5 seconds after Spoken goes quiet, Lore Forever continues where it left off.
- If Lore Forever starts a story by itself while Spoken is talking, it waits too.

Most Lore Forever stories are recorded in pieces, so they continue from the piece they stopped in. A story recorded as one single file starts over, because WoW can't start a sound partway through.

## Commands

- `/lfhush` turns it on or off
- `/lfhush on` and `/lfhush off` set it directly

## Requirements

Needs both **Spoken** and **Lore Forever**. Without either one it doesn't load.

Made for **WoW Forever**.
